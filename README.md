## Features
- Farming & cooking: Grow crops, prepare meals, and enjoy a slower side of Minecraft.
- Animals & pets: Discover wildlife, care for animals, and make room for some furry companions.
- Building & decoration: Create a cozy cottage, a countryside farm, a little shop, or an entire neighborhood.
- Exploration: Travel through the world, discover new landscapes, and find reasons to venture beyond your base.
- Multiplayer & community: Settle down with friends, trade resources, work on shared buildings, and watch your town grow over time.
